ABC HEALTH INSURANCE
MEDICAL NECESSITY POLICY — MAGNETIC RESONANCE IMAGING (MRI), LUMBAR SPINE
Policy Version: 2026.1
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Payer: ABC Health Insurance
Policy ID: ABC-MRI-LSPINE-2026

--- PAGE 1 ---
SECTION 1 — PURPOSE
This policy defines the medical necessity criteria that must be documented
in the medical record for coverage of Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) of
the lumbar spine (CPT 72148) for adult members.

SECTION 2 — SCOPE
This policy applies to outpatient and office-based referrals for lumbar
spine MRI where low back pain, radiculopathy, or suspected disc pathology
is the primary indication.

--- PAGE 2 ---
SECTION 3 — GENERAL COVERAGE PRINCIPLES
ABC Health Insurance covers advanced imaging services when the documented
clinical evidence demonstrates that the requested service is reasonable
and necessary for the diagnosis or treatment of the member's condition,
consistent with generally accepted standards of medical practice.

--- PAGE 7 ---
SECTION 4 — MEDICAL NECESSITY CRITERIA FOR LUMBAR SPINE MRI

4.1 Clinical Indication
The member must have a documented clinical indication such as low back
pain with or without radiculopathy, suspected disc herniation, spinal
stenosis, or neurological deficit referable to the lumbar spine.

4.2 Duration of Symptoms
Symptoms must have persisted for at least six (6) weeks prior to the
imaging request, unless red-flag findings are present (e.g., cauda equina
syndrome, progressive neurological deficit, suspected malignancy or
infection), in which case the six-week requirement is waived.

--- PAGE 8 ---
4.3 Conservative Treatment Requirement
Prior to advanced imaging, the member must have attempted a trial of
conservative (non-surgical) treatment appropriate to the condition. Accepted
conservative treatments include, but are not limited to:
  a) Physical therapy of at least four (4) weeks duration, OR
  b) A supervised home exercise program of comparable duration, AND
  c) A trial of pharmacologic therapy (e.g., NSAIDs or other analgesics),
     unless contraindicated.

4.4 Documented Treatment Failure
The medical record must document that conservative treatment failed to
produce clinically meaningful improvement, or that the member was
intolerant to conservative treatment.

--- PAGE 9 ---
4.5 Physician Documentation
A licensed physician must document, in the medical record, the clinical
rationale supporting the need for advanced imaging, including how the
results are expected to change management (e.g., referral for injection,
surgical evaluation, or definitive diagnosis).

SECTION 5 — EXCLUSIONS
This policy does not apply to trauma-related imaging performed within 72
hours of acute injury, which is governed by a separate emergent-imaging
policy (ABC-MRI-TRAUMA-2026).

--- PAGE 10 ---
SECTION 6 — DOCUMENTATION CHECKLIST
Claims submitted for lumbar spine MRI should include documentation
addressing each of the following:
  [ ] Duration and nature of symptoms
  [ ] Conservative treatment(s) attempted and duration
  [ ] Outcome of conservative treatment (response or failure)
  [ ] Physician's clinical rationale for advanced imaging
  [ ] Relevant physical exam / neurological findings, if available
